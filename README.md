# AI Meeting Room

A cozy meeting room where you chat with several AI friends at once, hold voice calls, and run Studio projects where the friends plan, create, review and deliver game avatars or short video edits together.

**Open it:** https://rriv97-create.github.io/ai-meeting-room/

## Privacy

- The whole app is one file, `index.html`, and it runs entirely in your browser.
- Your API keys are never stored in this project. They stay in your own browser, and each message goes straight from your browser to that AI company.
- Video clips and pictures you add in Studio stay on your device. Only small preview frames are sent to friends that can see pictures.
